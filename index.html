Guild roster & stats
Add each new video under a new YYYY-MM-DD key in data.js (NODE_WAR_DATA). Optional outcome is kept for your records only (not shown on this page). Weekly rolls up all days whose week (Sun–Sat, UTC) matches the week you pick. Monthly rolls up every war logged in that calendar month (UTC). Lifetime sums across every logged day (max kill streak and max cannon distance use the best single war; time dead and time survived add up across wars). Click any column header to sort (stats default to highest first; click again to reverse). Family name defaults to A→Z, then Z→A. The bottom row shows averages for everyone currently listed (if you filter by name, averages use only those rows).
